# How Much Does a Diving Vacation in Saba Cost?

A Saba diving vacation usually costs less like a simple activity purchase and more like a small expedition budget: international travel to St. Maarten, onward transportation, accommodation, diving, marine park contributions, meals, and a few island extras. The total varies widely because accommodation style, trip length, dive frequency, equipment rental, and transportation choices have a much larger effect than any single fee.

The most honest answer is therefore not a single number. It is a budgeting framework, plus current prices only where they are published by authoritative sources. Sea Saba's live diving rates and package availability are maintained in Checkfront rather than copied into static articles, so use the [booking page](/book) for current diving prices before finalizing a budget.

## The main cost categories

| Category | What affects the cost |
| --- | --- |
| International travel | Origin city, season, baggage, how far ahead you book |
| St. Maarten to Saba | Winair versus ferry, fare class, baggage, schedule |
| Accommodation | Hotel versus cottage or villa, season, room type, length of stay |
| Diving | Number of dive days, two-tank versus single dives, courses, private guide or charter |
| Equipment | Whether you bring personal gear or rent a full setup |
| Marine park and chamber contributions | Number of dives completed |
| Meals | Restaurants versus cooking, lunch and dinner choices |
| Local transportation | Taxis, rental vehicle, non-diving activities |
| Insurance and documents | Travel insurance, dive accident coverage, departure tax |

## Diving costs

Diving prices, package discounts, rental rates, availability, and inclusions are operational inventory details maintained in Checkfront. They can change, and copying them into an evergreen article would create a second pricing source that could become wrong.

Use Checkfront for current numbers, then consider these variables:

- A two-tank day usually provides better value than treating every dive as a separate decision.
- Multi-day diving may have package advantages.
- The Advanced and Classic trips serve different experience levels; do not choose by price alone.
- Private guides cost more but can be the right investment for newer divers, photographers, families, or mixed-experience groups.
- Courses have their own pricing and equipment inclusions.

Complimentary 32% Nitrox is included for certified Nitrox divers. That can reduce the marginal cost of using Nitrox compared with destinations that charge per fill, but certification itself is still required.

## Marine park and hyperbaric contributions

Sea Saba currently lists two externally set per-dive contributions:

- $3 per diver per dive for the Saba Marine Park
- $1 per diver per dive as a hyperbaric chamber contribution

Together, that is $4 per dive. A diver completing ten dives should budget $40 in these contributions. Snorkeling carries a $3 marine park contribution per person.

These amounts are small compared with the total trip cost, but they are important because they support the protection and emergency infrastructure that make Saba diving possible.

## Accommodation

Accommodation is often the largest controllable cost after international airfare. Saba has boutique hotels, cottages, and villas rather than large resorts, and prices vary by season, view, kitchen facilities, privacy, and room type.

One published example is Cottage Club, whose website has listed cottages starting around $160 in low season and $200 in high season, with higher penthouse pricing, before government tax and nature fee. That is only one property and is date-sensitive, so treat it as an illustration rather than an island-wide rate.

For groups, splitting a cottage or villa may cost less per person than separate hotel rooms. For solo travelers, a simple hotel room in Windwardside can reduce both cost and transportation friction.

## Transportation to and around Saba

The final connection from St. Maarten can be a meaningful part of the budget.

Makana Ferry's published fare matrix has shown adult Saba to St. Maarten round-trip pricing at $134, with the exact one-way amount depending on direction. Winair's fares are dynamic; recent route pages have shown one-way fares varying significantly by date. Both may include or add baggage rules differently, so compare the full cost rather than only the base fare.

On Saba, divers do not usually need a rental car because Sea Saba provides complimentary transportation for scheduled dive trips. Budget instead for taxis or occasional transportation if you plan restaurant evenings, hiking, or sightseeing. Some travelers still prefer the independence of a rental vehicle.

## Meals

Saba's restaurants are small, independent, and varied, but opening days change and supplies arrive by sea. You can control costs by choosing accommodation with a kitchen and preparing some breakfasts or lunches yourself.

A practical food budget depends on whether you:

- Cook breakfast and eat dinner out
- Eat most meals in restaurants
- Prefer casual cafés or multi-course dinners
- Buy snacks and drinks for dive days

Reservations are recommended for dinner, especially in busy periods.

## Small mandatory or likely expenses

- Saba airport or harbor departure tax: $10, with cash advised
- Accommodation nature fee: commonly $1 per guest per night where collected for trail upkeep
- Marine park and chamber contributions: $4 per dive
- Travel insurance and dive accident insurance: varies by traveler and coverage
- Baggage charges: varies by airline or ferry fare

## Illustrative budget structure

Because current diving, hotel, and flight prices must be verified at booking time, use this structure rather than trusting an outdated total:

| Budget item | Example calculation method |
| --- | --- |
| Diving | Current Checkfront price for planned trip × number of days |
| Marine contributions | $4 × planned number of dives |
| Accommodation | Nightly rate × nights + taxes/fees |
| Inter-island transport | Current Winair or Makana fare × travelers |
| Meals | Daily estimate × days, adjusted for kitchen use |
| Local transport | Planned taxi trips or rental vehicle |
| Insurance | Travel + dive accident coverage premium |
| Departure tax | $10 × travelers |

A careful traveler can often reduce cost by cooking some meals, sharing accommodation, bringing personal equipment, choosing ferry timing carefully, or traveling outside the busiest periods. Do not reduce the budget by skipping insurance or compressing the final flying-after-diving interval.

## The simple answer

A Saba diving vacation can be shaped to different budgets, but it is not a mass-market resort trip. The biggest costs are transportation and accommodation, while diving, food, and logistics depend on how many days you dive and how independently you travel.

Start with current diving availability and prices on the [booking page](/book), compare accommodation on [Plan Your Trip](/plan-your-trip), and use the cost categories above to build a realistic total before committing to flights.
