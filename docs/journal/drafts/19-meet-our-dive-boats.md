# Meet Our Dive Boats

Sea Saba's custom 38-foot Caribbean-built power catamarans are central to the dive experience. They are designed for the realities of Saba: short but sometimes exposed runs, repetitive daily diving, mixed groups, camera equipment, rental gear, and the need to move safely between sheltered and more demanding sites.

Published descriptions identify Fin & Tonic and Shark Bait as the operation's primary boats. Current public dive records also reference Northern Sky and private-boat entries, but the precise status of every vessel needs owner confirmation before publication.

## Designed for diving, not adapted as an afterthought

The boats are power catamarans with shaded seating, spacious decks, bow lounging areas, marine heads, large deep boarding ladders, camera tables, freshwater rinse tanks, freshwater hoses, and room for equipment. Fin & Tonic also has an upper observation deck.

These details are practical rather than decorative. Shade matters during repetitive tropical dive days. Stable decks matter when divers move in full equipment. Camera tables matter when guests travel with delicate housings. Large ladders matter when everyone is tired at the end of a second or third dive.

## Safety and support systems

Published boat features include emergency oxygen, first aid, spare equipment, and Save-a-Dive kits. Filtered drinking water and Gatorade are available aboard.

As with any safety system, the important part is not simply listing equipment. It is regular checking, crew familiarity, and readiness. Those operational details should be verified by the captains before publication.

## Short runs, real ocean

Most dive sites are short boat rides from Fort Bay Harbor. That means less time commuting and more time diving. It does not mean the ocean is always calm. Catamaran stability, thoughtful seating, and clear crew instructions all contribute to guest comfort.

## What needs owner confirmation

A publishable version of this article should confirm:

- Current fleet names and operational status
- Passenger and diver capacity
- Horsepower, cruising speed, and build details if appropriate
- Which features are present on each boat
- When boats were acquired, refitted, or renamed
- Whether Northern Sky remains part of the current fleet
- Approved photographs and photographer credits

## The simple answer

Sea Saba's boats are purpose-built tools for safe, comfortable, efficient diving in a real ocean environment. They are part dive platform, part classroom, part camera workspace, and part bridge between Fort Bay and the marine park.
