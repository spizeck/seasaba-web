## Consolidation decision

The requested topics `A First-Time Diver's Guide to Saba` and `Is Saba Suitable for Beginner Divers?` were consolidated into one article because they have the same search intent. Publishing both would create near-duplicate content and weaken topical authority. The combined draft is [Beginner Divers in Saba](drafts/10-beginner-divers-saba.md).
